Where the project stands

Read time: 4 minutes. One win: you can look at the ticket board and say, without guessing, what is proven, what is waiting, and what you are allowed to claim.

Rule of the house: an open ticket is not a broken feature. It is a gate that has not been passed. Closed means a test or a log exists. The difference between those two is the whole game.

0. The board in one picture

27 closed · 5 waiting on hardware or time · 4 parked UI · 3 waiting on people and process — 39 tickets total on 2026-11-26.

1. Done: what you can say

Framing and protocol — 01–09

Proof Both boards boot from one source, handshake, frame messages, and pass T2–T12 on hardware.

Crypto — 10–14, 31–34, 36, 38–39

Proof Encrypted DATA, replay and tamper rejection, forward secrecy, per-frame keys, ECDH-masked headers, BYE wipe, BSSID lock — all verified on the boards.

Resilience and audit — 18–20, 30

Proof Re-links after restarts, watchdog recovers a hang, and STATUS survives a power cycle.

The full evidence for each of those lines is in Lesson 003, the claims ledger.

3. The sentence test

Safe: “Two boards, encrypted messages, and tamper rejection verified on hardware.”

Unsafe: “Untethered all-day secure radio with a reviewed design.” Every word in that sentence is an open ticket.

4. Test yourself

Which ticket cannot be called “reviewed” until a real person signs off?
A) 17 field measurement   B) 37 independent review   C) 21 soak
B — 37. A machine pass is an aid, not an independent reviewer. The pull request is how a human signs off.
What finally closed ticket 20 after the flash writes were fixed?
A) A longer soak   B) A host test   C) A power cycle
C — A power cycle. A write happening once proves the mechanism; reading the record back after reboot proves persistence.
Which claim is safe to say out loud today?
A) Tamper rejected on hardware   B) Twenty-four hour soak passed   C) Header is AES encrypted
A — Tamper rejected on hardware. Ticket 14 proved it. Ticket 21 is still running, and the header is masked, not AES-encrypted.
How many tickets are still open?
A) Seven remain   B) Twelve remain   C) Twenty remain
B — Twelve remain. The board has 39 tickets: 27 closed, 12 open. The open ones wait on time, hardware, or people.
